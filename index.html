Pay with a Touch

Forget cash, forget cards. Just touch to pay.

How It Works

ThumbPay makes payments simple, fast, and secure using your fingerprint.

  • 1. Register your thumbprint: Create your ThumbPay account and securely register your fingerprint.
  • 2. Link your payment method: Add your bank account, debit card, or UPI ID to your ThumbPay wallet.
  • 3. Touch to pay: At a supported shop, place your finger on the scanner and confirm the payment.
  • 4. Get instant confirmation: Receive a payment confirmation on your phone immediately after the transaction.
